About Putt & Play

Where family fun meets adventure — discover our story, our passion, and the people behind the park.

Our Story

Born from a love of play

Putt & Play started in 2015 with a simple idea: create a place where families could putt, play, and laugh together — all under one roof.

What began as an 18-hole mini golf course has grown into a full family fun center, complete with a retro arcade, a family-friendly restaurant, and party rooms for celebrations of all kinds.

  • Over 5,000 happy families every year
  • Proudly local and independently owned
  • Committed to safe, clean, and inclusive fun
Our Mission

Fun for every generation

We believe that play is essential — not just for kids, but for everyone. Our mission is to create joyful experiences that bring families and friends together, away from screens and into the moment.

From grandparents to toddlers, every corner of our park is designed to be accessible, welcoming, and unforgettable. We're not just a mini golf course — we're a place where memories are made.

  • Inclusive pricing for families
  • Accessible facilities for all guests
  • Continuous reinvestment in new attractions
