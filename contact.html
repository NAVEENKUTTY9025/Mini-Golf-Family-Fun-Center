Quick Answers

Common Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? See our full FAQ.

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are always welcome! However, for groups of 10 or more or for weekend visits, we recommend booking online to guarantee your spot.

Is there parking available?

Yes! We have a large free parking lot on-site, including accessible spaces right near the entrance.

Are you family-friendly?

Absolutely. Our park is designed for all ages. We have age-appropriate equipment for toddlers and a family-friendly menu in the restaurant.

Can I host a birthday party?

Yes, we love celebrations! We offer party packages that include mini golf, arcade credits, food, and a private party room. Contact us for details.
