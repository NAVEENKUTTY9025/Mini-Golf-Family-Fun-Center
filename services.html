Our Services

From mini golf to parties, we've got everything you need for an unforgettable day of family fun.

What We Offer

Fun for every occasion

Six unique experiences, all under one roof.

18-Hole Mini Golf

Our signature adventure course features themed holes, water features, and challenging obstacles for all skill levels. Perfect for families, dates, and friendly competitions.

Open daily · 45–60 min play

Retro & Modern Arcade

Over 50 games, from classic pinball and air hockey to the latest racing simulators and claw machines. Win tickets and trade them for prizes at our reward counter.

Token & card system available

Family Restaurant

Enjoy burgers, pizza, salads, and kid-friendly favorites in our casual dining area. We also offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options for every appetite.

Dietary options available

Birthday Parties

Celebrate with a custom party package that includes mini golf, arcade credits, food, and a private party room. We handle the setup, you bring the smiles.

Packages for all ages

Corporate & Group Events

Host your team-building day, school field trip, or family reunion at Putt & Play. We offer group rates, reserved areas, and customizable activity packages.

Groups of 10+ welcome

Golf Lessons & Clinics

Improve your putting with one-on-one lessons or group clinics led by our PGA-trained instructors. Perfect for beginners and those looking to sharpen their skills.

All skill levels welcome
