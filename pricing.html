Good to Know

Pricing FAQs

Quick answers to common questions about our pricing.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes! For groups of 10 or more, we offer 15% off single passes. Contact our events team for a custom quote.

Are children under 3 free?

Children under 3 play mini golf for free when accompanied by a paying adult. Arcade credits are still required for games.

Can I upgrade my pass on-site?

Absolutely. If you start with a Single Pass and want to add arcade credits or snacks, just visit the front desk.

Do prices change on weekends?

No, our pricing is the same every day of the week. Special holiday events may have separate pricing.
