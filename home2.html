Our Journey

How we grew

From a single mini golf course to a full family destination.

2015

The First Hole

We opened our doors with a single 18-hole mini golf course and a big dream: to create a place where families could play together.

2017

Arcade Expansion

Added our retro arcade with classic games and modern favorites, giving guests even more reasons to stay and play.

2019

Family Restaurant

Opened our full-service restaurant, offering burgers, pizza, and family-friendly dining options for every appetite.

2022

Party & Event Spaces

Built private party rooms and event spaces to host birthdays, school trips, and corporate team-building days.

2024

5,000 Families Strong

Celebrated our 5,000th family visit — and we're just getting started. Here's to many more years of fun!
